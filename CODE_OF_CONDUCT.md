# Code of Conduct

## Expected conduct

Be respectful, specific, and patient. Critique work, not people.

Protect private information. Do not publish credentials, private paths, or user data.

Support evidence-based disagreement. Cite the applicable source and explain uncertainty.

## Unacceptable conduct

Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or share private information about another person.

Do not use project channels for unrelated promotion or repeated disruption.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers can edit, hide, or remove content that violates this policy. They can restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns through the private maintainer contact channel for the repository.
